Design Review — Rebrand

Glossa — Live Translation

Speak your language. Be understood in theirs.

Glossa (from Greek γλώσσα, meaning "tongue" or "language") is the new identity for the real-time speech translation platform formerly known as Rapport. This review documents the complete visual rebrand — new name, new design system, new layout — while preserving all 25 core features and the entire technical architecture unchanged.

00 Overview

Rebrand Summary

Rebrand scope: Visual identity and layout only. Zero features removed, zero added. All Zustand stores, services, audio pipeline, i18n keys, WebSocket connections, and behavioral tests remain untouched. The app is the same product with a fresh face.
Before — Rapport
Dark-first design

Navy backgrounds (#0B1120), cyan accent (#00B4D8), gradient bloom effects, glow rings. Four themes across two palettes (Navy, Rapport) × two grounds (Dark, Light).

After — Glossa
Light, open, approachable

White/cream surfaces (#FAFBFD), indigo accent (#6366F1), soft shadows, no bloom. Clean typography with Inter. Warm and professional, designed for long translation sessions without eye fatigue.

01 Brand

Brand Identity

Name & Etymology

Glossa (γλώσσα) — Ancient Greek for "tongue" and "language." The dual meaning captures both the physical act of speaking and the abstract concept of language, making it a natural fit for a speech translation product. Short, memorable, internationally pronounceable.

Brand Mark — "Bridge"

Evolution of the Rapport "Meet" chevrons. The Glossa mark retains the dual-chevron motif (speaker → listener) but rendered with softer proportions and the new indigo palette. The gap between chevrons symbolizes the translation bridge.

Standard (32px+)

Dual chevrons on indigo gradient rounded square. Speaker chevron at full opacity, listener at 70%. Stroke weight: 2.5px at 24px mark size.

Compact (<24px)

Single chevron only, thicker stroke. Minimum: 16px screen, 6mm print. Used in browser extension popup and system tray.

Wordmark

"Glossa" in Inter 700 with -0.03em tracking. Mark + wordmark lockup with 14px gap. Used in title bar and marketing surfaces.

Voice & Personality
Warm

Approachable and human. Translation is about connection, not technology. UI copy is conversational, never robotic.

Clear

No jargon in user-facing copy. Technical terms (ASR, VAD, TTS) stay in developer settings only. Labels are verbs or plain nouns.

Confident

Quiet confidence. No exclamation marks, no "awesome!" confirmations. The product works; the UI reflects that.

14 Internals

Accessibility

Contrast improvement: The Glossa light theme achieves higher contrast ratios than Rapport's dark themes for body text, while maintaining AAA compliance. All ARIA patterns, focus management, and keyboard navigation are preserved unchanged.

Keyboard (Unchanged)

  • Tab/Shift+Tab focus cycling (drawer trap)
  • Arrow keys for tabs (L/R/Home/End)
  • Space/Enter for toggle switches
  • Escape closes with nesting awareness

ARIA (Unchanged)

  • role="switch" + aria-checked
  • aria-current="page" on active rail
  • role="dialog" + aria-modal
  • aria-live="polite" on toasts
  • aria-pressed on segmented controls

Contrast (Glossa Light)

  • --ink on --bg: 16.8:1 (AAA)
  • --ink-2 on --bg: 10.7:1 (AAA)
  • --ink-3 on --bg: 5.6:1 (AA)
  • --accent on --bg: 4.6:1 (AA)
  • prefers-reduced-motion: respected

Custom title bar updated to white background. Subtitle as separate BrowserWindow. Auto-update. Virtual audio driver renamed: GlossaVirtualAudio. macOS .pkg installer with updated welcome/conclusion pages.
